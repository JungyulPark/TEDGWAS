# TED-TRAP v5: Week 1 Summary Memo

## 1. Week 1 Findings
* **pQTL Feasibility**: The availability of cis-pQTLs for TSHR, IGF1R, IGF1, and INSR in major plasma proteomics datasets (UKB-PPP and deCODE) is currently **PENDING**. This is not a limitation but a scheduled task for Week 2. At the start of Week 2, the exact panel manifestation lists will be downloaded and cross-referenced to definitively resolve all 8 target cells.
* **Fine-Mapping**: Exhaustive sensitivity LD clumping, GCTA-COJO conditional selection, and SuSiE-RSS Bayesian fine-mapping were performed on the TSHR cis-eQTL locus. 
* **Independent Signals**: Integration of frequentist and Bayesian models unequivocally demonstrated that the TSHR locus is dominated by a single major association signal. All secondary signals selected by COJO were either heavily influenced by LD reference mismatch (in EAS) or failed to pass strict SuSiE Credible Set and low-collinearity (r2 < 0.1) thresholds (in EUR).

## 2. Key Status Indicators
* **TSHR Single-IV Status**: **UNRESOLVED**. The TSHR locus remains a single-IV system represented by `rs179252`. A robust multi-IV MR design is not supported by the genetic architecture of the locus.
* **pQTL Feasibility**: **WEEK 2 PLANNED**. The status of the 8 target cells is pending verification against the UKB-PPP (Sun 2023) and deCODE (Ferkingstad 2021) panel lists. Because IGF1 is a secreted protein, it has a high probability of being present on the plasma panels; confirming its pQTL availability will be a mandatory early step in Week 2 to establish orthogonal evidence.

## 3. Recommended Journal Tier & Next Steps
Based on the TED-TRAP v5 ceiling decision logic, relying solely on a single-IV MR for TSHR without novel discoveries severely limits the publication ceiling (Front Endocrinol / JEI / Sci Rep). 

To achieve a higher-tier target (**IOVS / JCEM / EBioMedicine exploratory**), the study must transition its framing from a simple "TSHR confirmation" to a **"Druggable-gene-wide genetic prioritization"**. 

**Recommendation:** Proceed immediately to **Week 2-3 systematic Druggable-Genome-Wide MR**. Only by discovering and replicating novel druggable hits beyond the TSHR locus can the study satisfy the methodological and novelty requirements of high-impact journals.

Table 1. Study design and data sources
Role Dataset Ancestry Sample size / cases
Exposure (instruments) eQTLGen blood cis-eQTL Predominantly European up to 31,684 participants
Discovery (primary) BBJ Graves disease (GCST90018627) East Asian 2,809 cases
Replication UKB hyperthyroidism (GCST90038636) European 3,731 cases
Sensitivity (TED-specific) FinnGen R12 Graves ophthalmopathy European 858 cases
Target tissue In-house orbital RNA-seq Korean 4 TED + 1 control
eQTLGen sample size indicates the maximum available blood cis-eQTL sample size; SNP-level sample sizes vary across instruments (e.g., rs179252 NrSamples = 31,566). BBJ Graves disease was used as the primary discovery phenotype, UKB hyperthyroidism as cross-ancestry/broader-phenotype replication, and FinnGen Graves ophthalmopathy as the TED-specific sensitivity outcome. UKB binary-trait effect estimates were rescaled from linear scale to log-odds using case prevalence.

Verification of whether Paper 1's approach/claim is already published. Searches
run across 4 angles (druggable MR GD/TED; eQTL/pQTL coloc GD-GO 2024–25; the
susceptibility-vs-effector framing; orbital-tissue MR cross-ancestry).

  (multiple 2025 papers). E.g. *Sci Rep* 2025 multi-omics MR (mQTL/eQTL/pQTL),
  validates **TSHR** among 4 genes in FinnGen Graves ophthalmopathy with coloc.
  → confirms the locked rule "druggable GD MR is already published; no first claim."

  (*ImmunoTargets Ther* 2025: CD40/TINAGL1/GMPR/CXCL10 for GD; BTN1A1/FCRL1 for GO;
  plus a proteome-wide TED MR, 9 targets, FCRL1/BDNF validated).
  ⚠️ **Affects Paper 2:** the pQTL-MR-for-GD/GO space is taken. Paper 2 must frame
  its pQTL work *narrowly as an INSR/off-target axis*, NOT as novel target discovery.
- **Orbital tissue RNA-seq in TED, incl. IGF-1R pathway,** is published (Sci Rep 2025
  orbital transcriptome; bioRxiv 2024 "TED orbital fat adipogenicity and IGF-1R
  pathway"; TAO scRNA-seq). → our tissue data type is not novel; keep it exploratory.

## What appears NOT yet published (our defensible niche)

  from *IGF1R as a non-colocalized pharmacologic effector* ("a drug target that is not
  a susceptibility locus"). Targeted IGF1R-MR searches returned no such framing.
- The **combination** in one paper: cross-ancestry 3-outcome hierarchy (BBJ + UKB +
  FinnGen) + CTLA4 positive control + TSHR single-IV fine-mapping defense + own
  orbital tissue triangulation, used to separate susceptibility from effector.

## Honest verdict

  contribution") is accurate and confirmed.
- Caveat: absence of a matching hit ≠ proof none exists; this is diligence, not certainty.

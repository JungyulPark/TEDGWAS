# Submission venue cascade (internal)

Profile of this manuscript: **high methodological rigor, low empirical novelty**
(no novel target; value is the conceptual susceptibility-anchor vs effector-axis
framework). Prior desk rejects: *Thyroid*, *European Thyroid Journal* (both
narrow thyroid-specialist editor pools). Plan venues so the framing fits the
editor pool, and pre-decide the fallback so no time is lost between rejections.

| Tier | Venue | Why it fits | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **J. Endocrinological Investigation (JEI)** | Broad endocrine + translational + genetic epi; conceptual hook reads to a general endocrine editor; current target. | "preferably 4,500" words; fast/aggressive desk filter (median ~8 d); MR fatigue. |
| **2** | **Endocrine Connections** (open access, ESE/SfE) | Sound-science friendly; rigorous-but-incremental MR is in scope; lower novelty bar. | Lower impact; APC. |
| **3** | **Genes (MDPI)** / **Frontiers in Genetics or Endocrinology** | Genetics/translational audience where the "framework / cautionary" framing sells best; tolerant of negative/conservative results. | MDPI/Frontiers reputation concerns; APC; reviewer variance. |
| **alt** | **Thyroid Research** / **BMC Endocrine Disorders** | Sound-science, methods-first; the honest robust_novel=0 is an asset. | Lower visibility. |

## Framing lever (applies most at Tier 1)
Lead the cover letter with the **framework / cautionary** thesis
(genetic susceptibility ≠ drug target; TSHR anchors liability, IGF1R is an
effector exploited by teprotumumab). Because empirical novelty is null, a
consistent "framework + rigorous negative screen" identity reads stronger to a
reviewer than a thin novelty claim.

## Decision rule
- JEI desk reject → go straight to Tier 2 (Endocrine Connections), do not re-shop
  to another thyroid-specialist journal (same editor pool that already rejected).
- JEI major revision → answer, resubmit; keep the external-GEO note ready as
  honest rebuttal material if a reviewer asks about external replication.
- If two non-thyroid venues also bounce on novelty → consider repositioning as a
  methods/cautionary note, or fold into a larger study (see
  `STRENGTHENING_AND_NOVELTY_OPTIONS.md`).

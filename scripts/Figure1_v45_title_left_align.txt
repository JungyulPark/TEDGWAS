Figure 1의 schematic title을 가운데에서 → 왼쪽으로 이동.

방법 A) PowerPoint에서 직접: 
   Title text box를 드래그해서 좌측 상단으로 이동
   (text-align: left)

방법 B) Image LLM에 다음 prompt 입력:

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Please update the attached scientific workflow diagram (Figure 1)
with the following layout change ONLY. Keep all other elements
(boxes, arrows, content, INTEGRATION text, etc.) exactly the same.

LAYOUT CHANGE: Move the figure title from center to LEFT alignment.

If the figure currently has a title at the top (e.g., "Figure 1.
Study design and analytical workflow"):
- Move it to the LEFT side of the top margin
- Change text alignment to LEFT-ALIGN
- Match the style of journal figures where panel titles are
  left-aligned (e.g., "(A) ...", "(B) ...")

Do not change anything else. Output: PNG, same dimensions
as input (~4900 × 2800 px), high resolution.
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

방법 C) Figure 1을 그대로 두고 Manuscript caption만 의존:
   → ScholarOne에서 figure caption은 manuscript에 있으므로
     실제로 figure 자체에 title이 없어도 됨
   → 가장 간단한 옵션

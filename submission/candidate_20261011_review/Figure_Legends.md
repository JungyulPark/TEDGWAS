# Figure legends

**Figure 1. Study design and gene-level discovery in BBJ.** (A) Analysis flow. Blood expression instruments from eQTLGen were tested against BBJ GD for discovery; UKB hyperthyroidism and FinnGen GO were the original comparison datasets, and FinnGen GD, FinnGen GO strict and the GTEx tissue extension were added post hoc. The three FinnGen endpoints overlap and are not independent replications. (B) Each grey point is one estimable gene (n = 2,234), placed at the GRCh37 position of its strongest selected expression instrument and plotted by its BBJ MR *P* value; points are genes, not single variants. Filled black points are the 13 genes that passed *P* < 0.05/2,544 (dashed line, −log~10~*P* ≈ 4.71); the open circle is the focal gene *IGF1R*, which did not. The threshold applies to the BBJ discovery screen, not to later exploratory analyses. GTEx, Genotype-Tissue Expression; MR, Mendelian randomization; SNP, single-nucleotide polymorphism; BBJ, Biobank Japan; FinnGen GD, E4_GRAVES_STRICT; GD, Graves disease; GO, Graves ophthalmopathy; UKB, UK Biobank.

**Figure 2. *TSHR*, *IGF1R* and *CTLA4* across five disease datasets.** (A) MR odds ratios with 95% CIs per approximately 1 SD higher genetically predicted blood expression: Wald ratio for one instrument, multiplicative random-effects IVW for several. The axis is logarithmic and the dashed line is OR = 1. *TSHR* used one instrument, rs179252, in every dataset. Colours identify datasets. (B) Colocalization posterior probabilities for the same comparisons (p1 = p2 = 10^−4^, p12 = 10^−5^), stacked from zero as H4 (one shared variant), H3 (distinct variants), H2 (expression signal only) and H0/H1 (neither trait, or disease only). The dotted line marks PP.H4 = 0.80, the descriptive reporting threshold. Estimates, *P* values and posterior probabilities are given in Table 2; *P* values and posterior probabilities answer different questions. FinnGen GO controls include people with GD but no recorded eye disease. CI, confidence interval; IVW, inverse-variance weighted; MR, Mendelian randomization; OR, odds ratio; PP, posterior probability; SD, standard deviation; BBJ, Biobank Japan; FinnGen GD, E4_GRAVES_STRICT; GD, Graves disease; GO, Graves ophthalmopathy; UKB, UK Biobank.

**Figure 3. Colocalization across 24 selected genes and *TSHR* prior sensitivity.** (A) PP.H4 for each gene and dataset (p1 = p2 = 10^−4^, p12 = 10^−5^); shading follows the colour bar. Boxed cells have PP.H4 ≥ 0.80, and a dot marks cells that remain ≥ 0.80 at p12 = 10^−6^. Horizontal lines separate *TSHR*, *IGF1R* and *CTLA4*; the other 11 BBJ discoveries; the nine additional FinnGen GD discoveries; and *HLA-G*, added because its MR result changed with the allele-frequency source. Only these 24 genes underwent colocalization. Values below 0.001 are shown as <0.001; full-precision values are in the Supplementary Dataset. Low BBJ PP.H4 for the two *IGLV* genes partly reflects absence of the lead expression variant from the BBJ data. (B) *TSHR* PP.H4 in each dataset at three shared-variant priors (p12); points are offset slightly to avoid overlap, and the dashed line marks 0.80. PP.H4 is a posterior probability under the colocalization model, not a *P* value. MR, Mendelian randomization; PP.H4, posterior probability of one shared causal variant; BBJ, Biobank Japan; FinnGen GD, E4_GRAVES_STRICT; GD, Graves disease; GO, Graves ophthalmopathy; UKB, UK Biobank.

**Figure S1. Descriptive orbital expression of *TSHR*, *IGF1R* and *CTLA4*.** Transcript abundance in orbital adipose tissue collected during orbital decompression from four patients with inactive TED, and in nasal fat pad from one person without thyroid disease undergoing cosmetic upper blepharoplasty. Points are biological samples (technical replicates combined); horizontal lines show TED means. The log~2~ fold change is log~2~[(mean TED TPM + 0.01)/(control TPM + 0.01)]. Vertical axes differ by gene. The control came from a different fat depot, and one control cannot estimate control-group variance; no inferential test was performed, and these observations do not validate the genetic findings. TED, thyroid eye disease; TPM, transcripts per million.

**Figure S2. Dependence of *IGF1R* and *CTLA4* estimates on individual instruments.** For each dataset, the open diamond is the estimate using all instruments (as in Table 2), and the filled points below it omit the named instrument (IVW when at least two instruments remain, Wald ratio when one remains). Lines are 95% CIs on a logarithmic axis; the dashed line is OR = 1. *P* values are shown at right; bold, nominal *P* < 0.05. (A) *IGF1R* in all five datasets. (B) *CTLA4* in the four FinnGen and UKB datasets; BBJ *CTLA4* and all *TSHR* estimates use one instrument and could not be assessed. The 25 post hoc omission estimates reuse the same association data and are not independent replications; loss of nominal significance can reflect reduced precision as well as the omitted variant's contribution. CI, confidence interval; IV, instrumental variable; IVW, inverse-variance weighted; OR, odds ratio; SD, standard deviation; BBJ, Biobank Japan; FinnGen GD, E4_GRAVES_STRICT; GD, Graves disease; GO, Graves ophthalmopathy; UKB, UK Biobank.
